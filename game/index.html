Solmar
AN OPEN-WORLD CRIME STORY

Sun, salt air and bad decisions. Dario "Daz" Ferran came to Solmar to drive straight — until his old friend Lena Cross called in a debt and Viktor Hale's syndicate started squeezing the whole city. Steal cars, outrun the Solmar PD, run six jobs across the city and take Hale down. The city is yours to explore: drive it, walk it, own it.

ON FOOT

WASD move, Shift sprint, Space jump. Click the game to lock the mouse and look around. Click fires your pistol, F punches, R reloads.

DRIVING

Walk up to any car and press E to get in — parked cars are free, but taking one the city owns raises your wanted level. W accelerates, S brakes and reverses, Space is the handbrake. Press E again to get out.

THE HEAT

Crime raises your wanted stars (1–5). Police cars chase you and officers on foot will arrest you if they catch you. Stay out of sight and out of trouble and the heat cools off. Green crosses heal you, yellow crates refill ammo.

MISSIONS

Follow the golden beam and the blip on the minimap. Finish all six missions to take Hale down. Progress and cash save automatically — and with P → Save.

Solmar is an original game made for make.fun gta6-01. No network requests — three.js is vendored locally and all sound is synthesized in code. Best played in desktop Chrome with keyboard and mouse.
